The building through the day

Not a headcount, and it should not be read as one. Two cameras watching one place count the same person twice, somebody at a desk no camera covers is not counted at all, and a corridor camera counts whoever walks past. What it is good for is the shape: when the building fills, when it empties, and whether today looks like yesterday.
